CONCLUSION
  The null on defense burden is not hiding combination effects. Of the 135 pairs with enough strains in every carriage combination to support an interaction term, none shows one. Counting systems fails to predict lysis, and so does pairing them.
  Neither test reaches three-way or higher combinations. With 402 strains and this much correlation between defense repertoires, those cells are too thinly populated to estimate, and no amount of modelling recovers information the collection does not contain.
