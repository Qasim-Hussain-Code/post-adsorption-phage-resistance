  No system passes all four stricter tests. That is the honest summary and it is not a failure of the analysis: each test discards most of the data in order to remove a different source of confounding, so none has much power left. Two patterns are worth separating.
    RM_Type_IIG holds up when carriage is permuted inside a phylogroup (q = 3.30e-03) but its effect disappears among near-identical genomes (OR 0.968, 7 discordant clusters). The within-cluster test had moderate power here, so this is evidence against, not merely absence of evidence.
    Zorya_TypeII keeps its effect size among near-identical genomes (OR 0.344 against 0.444 adjusted) but only 2 clusters are informative, so the interval is wide and the test is uninformative rather than negative. It does not reach significance under permutation either.
    RM_Type_III, Retron_I_C, RosmerTA did not survive the lineage-adjusted regression but are significant and protective in at least one stricter design. They are not claims, but they are the systems most worth a targeted experiment.
