  Observing no lysis among the carriers is therefore exactly what the null predicts. The carriers are few and the phages involved barely lyse anything, so this comparison cannot distinguish superinfection exclusion from chance however it is analysed. The result below is reported for completeness and carries no weight.

  One thing this does establish. The phages found integrated in host genomes are 411_P1, 411_P2, and 2 of 2 of them were independently classified temperate in script 17 on the basis of carrying a tyrosine integrase. Two analyses that share no input beyond the phage genomes themselves, one searching host chromosomes for phage sequence and one searching phage proteomes for integration machinery, pick out the same phages. That is a real check on both.

  The regression cannot be fitted at all: with eight exposed pairs and no lysis among them the likelihood has no interior maximum. The honest summary is that this collection cannot test superinfection exclusion, and the reason is in its design rather than in the method used here. Every one of the eight pairs is non-lytic, which is consistent with the mechanism and equally consistent with the 0.74 percent baseline lysis rate of the two phages involved.

  That last correlation is the one substantive result in this section. Strains richer in site-specific recombinases carry more defense systems, which is what is expected if defense systems travel on mobile elements. It also means defense carriage is partly a marker of how much mobile DNA a strain has taken up, which is one more thing that a defense system can be standing in for in the associations reported elsewhere in this analysis.
  Strain-level counts are between-strain comparisons and inherit every confound that script 04 removed. They are descriptive; the within-strain contrast above is the one to read.

LIMITS
  Exact k-mer containment detects prophages closely related to a collection phage and misses diverged ones: at k = 21 a prophage at 95 percent nucleotide identity retains only about a third of its k-mers. This measures relatedness to these 96 phages, not prophage content in general, which is the right target for superinfection exclusion but not for prophage-encoded defense in general.
  The analysis covers 135 of 402 strains, and strains deposited in public archives are not a random sample of the collection.
  The binding limitation is not method but collection design. Prophages closely related to a collection phage are rare here because the Guelin phages were chosen for lytic activity: only two of the ninety-six are temperate, and those two plaque on under one percent of strains. Superinfection exclusion needs a temperate phage that also forms plaques, and this panel contains almost none. Testing the mechanism properly needs a collection assembled for that question, not a reanalysis of one assembled for another.
  Containment is measured from an assembly. A prophage split across contigs is still detected, since k-mers are counted without regard to assembly structure, but a prophage absent from the assembly through collapse of a repeated region would be missed.
