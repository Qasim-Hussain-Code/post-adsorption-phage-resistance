WHY THIS SUPERSEDES THE DECOMPOSITION IN SCRIPT 03
  Script 03 added blocks in one fixed order and read off the increments. WHAT CHANGES
  Host surface now accounts for 0.0830 of pseudo R2 against 0.0269 for all 33 defense systems together, a ratio of 3.1 to 1. The same ordering holds for the block fitted alone (0.1010 against 0.0410) and for the block added last (0.0685 against 0.0179), so it does not depend on how the shared variation is allocated.
  Per term the ordering reverses: surface contributes 0.00053 per term against 0.00081 for defense, so defense is the more efficient block by a factor of 1.5. The surface advantage in total comes partly from spending 157 terms against 33. Both readings are reported because they answer different questions: how much of the variation a block accounts for, and how much each term buys.
  The earlier finding is therefore reversed in total terms. Once the surface block contains what this collection actually types about the cell surface, and once the comparison no longer depends on the order the blocks are entered, host surface accounts for substantially more of the variation in lysis than intracellular defense does. That agrees with the published analysis of the same dataset, which reported adsorption-related features as more informative than antiphage systems, and it contradicts what script 03 implied.
  Which phage is being tested remains the largest single term (0.1920, 60 percent of the explained variation), and clonal lineage contributes 0.0199.

  Two cautions. These are descriptive shares of explained variation, not causal effects, and the blocks remain correlated with one another however the shared part is allocated. And a block with more terms can capture more variation simply by being more flexible, which is why the per-term column is shown beside the totals rather than instead of them.

  The conclusion that intracellular defense explains little of the variation in lysis is unchanged and is in fact strengthened: 33 defense systems together account for 8 percent of the explained variation, and almost all of the associations behind that share do not survive control for clonal background.
