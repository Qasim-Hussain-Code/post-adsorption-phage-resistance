READING THIS ALONGSIDE THE EARLIER RESULTS
  Shrinkage is a different question from the robustness tests in script 09, and it is weaker. It corrects estimates for the fact that many systems were examined; it does nothing about confounding by clonal background, which is what actually removed most of the associations. A system can survive shrinkage and still be a lineage artefact.
  The practical conclusion is unchanged. Across all 79 systems the pooled association with lysis is an odds ratio of 0.923, close to no association, and the systems that stand out individually are the same ones script 09 showed do not survive the stricter designs.
