WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT FIX
  It fixes the interval widths. It does nothing about confounding: the inputs are the lineage-adjusted estimates from script 13, and a system whose adjustment was inadequate is pooled just as confidently as one whose adjustment was sound. Pooling corrects for having looked at many systems, not for having measured each one in an observational collection.
  The prior on tau matters least where it matters most to be careful: with 79 estimates the data identify tau well, and the posterior interval 0.205 to 0.371 is narrow relative to the half-Cauchy prior it sits inside.
