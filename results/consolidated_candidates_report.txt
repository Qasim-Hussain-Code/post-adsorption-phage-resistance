READING THE LIST
  6 systems pass at least two independent checks: RM_Type_IIG, RM_Type_III, Shango, Lamassu-Cap4_nuclease, Zorya_TypeII, AbiD.
  No system passes all five, and that is the honest headline. The checks are not redundant: each discards most of the data to remove a different source of confounding, so each has little power left, and they disagree. A system passing two of five is a candidate worth an experiment, not a finding.

  2 candidates have fewer than three informative clonal clusters (Zorya_TypeII, PD-T7-4), so the within-cluster column for them is uninformative rather than negative. Failing a test that cannot be run is not evidence against a system.

  3 candidates are carried by fewer than 20 strains (Lamassu-Cap4_nuclease, AbiD, PD-T7-4). They reach this list only through the shrinkage model, their individual estimates are heavily pulled toward the pooled mean, and they should be treated as the weakest entries here despite their rank.

WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT
  Nothing in this dataset can. Every analysis here is observational and the confounding between a defense system and the lineage carrying it cannot be broken by stratifying a fixed collection more finely. The decisive experiment is to delete the system in its native host and challenge the isogenic pair with the phages this collection says it resists. The ranking above is a priority order for doing that, and the within-cluster column indicates which systems already have naturally occurring near-isogenic pairs in the collection to start from.
