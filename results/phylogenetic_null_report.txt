INTERPRETATION, REVISED
  Removing surface type from the outcome drops the impossible associations from 4 to 2. Most of what survived conditioning on ancestry alone was host surface composition, which is what the variance partition in script 11 would predict.

  RM_Type_IIG deserves separate comment. It passes the matched null on raw lysis (q = 3.30e-03) and its carriage is barely lineage-confined (phylogenetic R2 0.090), which is why it survives tests that condition on ancestry. But it is strongly depleted in strains with a permissive outer core (odds ratio 0.33, p = 4.0e-04): its carriers are 37 percent permissive against 60 percent of strains overall, so they are drawn from strains that are intrinsically harder to lyse whatever they carry.
  Removing surface type from the outcome attenuates the difference by 31 percent but it remains significant (q = 8.80e-03). So part of its apparent protection is the surface composition of its carriers and part is not. This is the one piece of evidence for RM_Type_IIG that survives conditioning on both ancestry and surface type without relying on a covariate-adjusted regression.
  It still fails the within-cluster test, which holds the entire genomic background fixed and had moderate power for this system. The two designs disagree, and neither is decisive: the within-cluster test uses 36 strains in 7 clusters, while this one uses all 402 but cannot rule out a confounder it has not been given.

  Adjusting for surface type also brings Zorya_TypeII across the threshold. Surface composition was masking these rather than creating them: their carriers sit disproportionately on outer cores that are hard to lyse anyway, so the raw comparison understated them.

  This test is conservative and has a known cost: a defense system genuinely confined to one lineage and genuinely protective is indistinguishable here from a passenger of that lineage. CAS_Class1-Subtype-I-E is the clearest case, with 0.59 of its carriage explained by core-genome position; this test cannot say whether it protects. Only deleting the system in its native background can.
