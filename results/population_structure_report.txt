  The associations pointing the wrong way provide an internal check on the adjustment, and it passes. A defense system cannot make its host easier to lyse, so every significant association with increased lysis must be confounding. Before lineage adjustment there were 2 such associations in this regression and 14 under the stratified estimates of script 03. After adjusting for phylogroup and core-genome position there are none. The covariates are therefore removing the kind of signal they were introduced to remove, which makes the attenuation of the protective associations harder to dismiss as overcorrection.
